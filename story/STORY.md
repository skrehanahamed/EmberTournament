## Prologue

When the last dragon died, its heart broke into five Ember Cores. Whoever holds all five can call the dragon's fire back.

Every hundred years, the Ember Tournament decides who is worthy. This year, the invitations were sent by the Hollow Emperor, whom nobody has ever seen.

Reno never wanted to be a fighter. He ran a small repair shop in a quiet town.

One rainy night, while he was fixing a broken radio, an envelope slid under his door. Black wax. A stamp in the shape of an R.

"You have been chosen. The Ember Tournament begins at dawn."  Reno laughed, and threw it in the bin.

By morning it was back on his workbench. And his right hand was glowing violet.

Far across the valley, the rooftops of his town were on fire.  Reno took a breath.  "Okay. Fine. I'll go."

## Epilogue

The five Cores rise and settle in Reno's glowing hand. He does not keep them.

One by one, he gives each fighter what they came for. Kaito learns his master's name is carved inside the Core: his master stayed, to keep the dragon's heart whole.

Valeria feels rain on her face for the first time in years. Mira hears the storms go quiet.

Brutus hears the crowd chanting, and decides he would rather have friends than a throne. Rudra watches the river turn back toward the sea.

The glow fades from Reno's hand. He walks home, picks up the radio, and finishes fixing it.

On the workbench, a new envelope waits, sealed in gold wax:  "To the next Keeper: Reno."
