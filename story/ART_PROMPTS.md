# Story cutscene art - prompts

Generate each picture in ChatGPT at **16:9 landscape (1536x1024 or bigger)**, save it as `Assets/story/<key>.png`
(exact key names below), then run `make story-art`. Any key you skip falls back to the stage picture with a slow camera drift.
Keep the bottom third of each picture fairly dark/empty - the subtitles go there.

**Style block (paste at the start of every prompt):**
> Cinematic 16-bit pixel art illustration, wide 16:9 landscape, detailed hand-drawn pixel shading, dramatic lighting, rich colours, fighting-game cutscene style (like a Street Fighter / Metal Slug story screen). No text, no letters, no watermark, no UI. Dark empty area in the lower third of the picture.

**Characters (keep them consistent):** Reno - young man, short black hair, plain work jacket with rolled sleeves, tool belt, a glowing violet letter R mark on his right hand. Kaito - calm swordsman monk, broken sword hilt. Valeria - sharp thief with a dagger, hooded. Mira - storm mage, silver hair, lightning. Brutus - huge scarred brawler. Rudra - Bengali fighter, river-wet clothes. The Hollow Emperor - a dark copy of Reno made of black-violet fire.
